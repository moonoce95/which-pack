Dual-pack outdoor tools: when a mower or trimmer needs two batteries

A kit with one spare pack looks fine until you buy outdoor gear. On our AU matrix, several mowers and line trimmers need two 18V packs at once. That is a coverage trap, even when the cell is scored has.
