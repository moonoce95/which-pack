SDS-MAX vs SDS-plus in Australia: the DeWalt FlexVolt trap

SDS-plus and SDS-MAX are not the same chuck. On DeWalt AU, the SDS-MAX hammers we sampled sit on 54V FlexVolt. The 18V XR rotary hammers on that catalog are SDS-plus. Buying an XR kit does not cover the SDS-MAX row.

Coverage trap Different chuck, different voltage on DeWalt AU

  • DeWalt AU SDS-MAX sampled: 54V FlexVolt (DCH614N-XJ). Scored missing on 18V XR and has on FlexVolt.
  • DeWalt AU SDS-plus on XR: sampled DCH133N-XJ. Separate FlexVolt SDS-plus exists (DCH333NT-XJ). That is still SDS-plus, not SDS-MAX.

Why this bites kit buyers

Starter kits sell SDS-plus hammers for brick and light concrete. Year two on slab or demo often wants SDS-MAX. On DeWalt AU that jump is also a voltage jump. FlexVolt packs can run XR tools. An XR pack cannot run a 54V-only SDS-MAX machine.
