Before you buy a cordless kit in Australia, check year-two tools

The kit box is drills, impacts, maybe a circular saw. Year two is when you want a table saw, track saw, SDS-MAX, or a mower. That is where platforms diverge in Australia.

One finding

Buy for the tools you will need after the kit, not just what is in the foam. On our AU matrix, some common year-two types are missing on the 18V line you thought you were buying.

Examples from the matrix

  • Jobsite table saw. Makita LXT and DeWalt 18V XR are missing. DeWalt cordless sits on FLEXVOLT 54V. Milwaukee M18 and Ryobi ONE+ are has.
  • Track / plunge saw. DeWalt AU cordless sampled is FLEXVOLT, scored missing on 18V XR.
  • SDS-MAX. DeWalt AU sampled on FLEXVOLT. 18V XR hammers on the catalog are SDS-plus.
  • Outdoor. Some Milwaukee and Makita trimmers/mowers need two 18V packs. Check the notes before you size a kit for one battery.

Verified 2026-09-03 from manufacturer AU pages. We do not invent cells.

What to do

List the year-two tools you actually want. Open the matrix. If a must-have is missing or only on a higher-voltage line, factor that in before you commit packs.
