Important Voltage and FlexVolt traps

  • US chargers are usually 110–120V. AU is 230–240V. A plug adapter is not a transformer.
  • DeWalt AU 18V XR is the same cell family often sold as 20V MAX in the US. Local packs use -XJ / -XE SKUs.
  • DeWalt’s AU cordless table saw, track/plunge saw, SDS-MAX, and 230mm+ chop saw in this matrix are 54V FlexVolt, not 18V XR.
  • Some mowers and trimmers need two 18V packs. Check the notes before you buy.
  • Ryobi ONE+ in AU is Bunnings-only. Shown for catalog honesty (not sold via our Amazon links).
